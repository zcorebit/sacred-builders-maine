Our Story

Built In Unity,
Maine

Sacred Builders, LLC is based in Unity, Maine and has been serving local homeowners and businesses since 2021. The company focuses on roofing and exterior services for residential and commercial properties throughout the Central Maine region.

Sacred Builders LLC provides dependable roofing and exterior services for homeowners and businesses throughout Unity, ME and surrounding Central Maine communities. Whether you need roof repairs, a full roof replacement, siding installation, or commercial roofing services, our experienced team delivers professional craftsmanship and honest pricing.

From small repairs to full exterior upgrades, Sacred Builders is committed to protecting your home with durable materials and skilled installation. As a locally owned Maine business, we take pride in serving our community with dependable, honest workmanship.

"Our goal is simple — deliver roofing and exterior work that lasts."
